## How to choose an actor

- **Monthly users** is the best single signal: it counts distinct people who ran the actor in the last 30 days, so abandoned tools sink to the bottom.
- **Rating** comes from Apify Store reviews. A 4.5+ rating with dozens of reviews means the output is reliable.
- **Pricing model** differs per actor (pay per result, pay per event, or a monthly rental). The live price is always shown on the actor page, so compare there before a big run.
- **Run a small test first.** Limit the input to 10 or 20 results, check the fields you need, then scale up.
